# New additions & changes — Oct 26 (Lady D)

New as of this morning, to review with Dennys. Items 1–2 are renames (need team OK);
3–6 are new design.

## Renames (pending team agreement)
1. **"The Climb" → "The Journey."** Reasoning: the path isn't a straight climb — it's
   squiggly, with ups, downs and turns. "Journey" fits better. (Presentation chart already updated.)
2. **"Greeter" → "Check-In Console."** At Pursuit we check in at a console, not with a
   person. Same job (self check-in → the *Show Up* star; welcome shows on screen). Nothing lost.

## New character
3. **Program Manager** — a visible character who **directs the class**.
   - Distinct from the **Orchestrator** (which stays the invisible system coordinator that
     routes agents and verifies handoffs).
   - Flow: Program Manager leads/introduces the class → sends the student into Mr. P's room.

## Mr. P's special room
4. **A dedicated purple room** (shades of purple) that is Mr. P's space, with a
   **laptop-style screen**. The screen shows a **series of questions driven by the student's level**
   (a **question bank per level** we author and grow).
5. **Question progression (per day).** Each day has a **set number of questions** (drawn from
   that level's question bank).
   - **Wrong answer:** Mr. P is **persistent** — he does *not* just say pass/fail; he **re-asks
     (at least 5 times)**, rephrasing/pressing, until the student gets it right.
   - **Correct answer:** the student is **acknowledged** ("Nice — that's it!") and **advances to
     the next question** for that day.
   - Completing the **whole day's set** = that day is done (and acknowledged).
   - Loop: *ask → answer → (wrong: re-ask · right: acknowledge + next) → repeat until the day's
     questions are complete.*
6. **Dial-a-Friend (AI friends).** A box with a **☎ telephone symbol** labeled "Dial a Friend."
   Clicking it lists AI assistants to call — **Gemini, Claude, Codex, ChatGPT** (+ at least one
   more, e.g. Perplexity / Copilot / Llama). This is the **AI Helper** mechanic made literal, and
   it lives inside Mr. P's room. Help is **guidance only** — it can't hand over the star.

## Open questions to decide together
- Does each wrong re-ask cost a little **energy** (reason to call a friend), or stay pressure-free?
- A **graceful floor**: after ~5 tries, flash Dial-a-Friend / bigger hint so a student is pushed
  toward AI but never truly stuck (matches our "a failure shouldn't block completion" rule).
- Which **5th AI friend** to include.
- **Program Manager vs. Orchestrator** scope — keep fully separate, or let PM carry the "directing" feel.
